Theme Studio: an interactive builder that derives a complete, accessible colour theme from one base and one brand colour. It opens preselected with Apple Cool, warmwind blue #0071E3, a light sidebar, subtle intensity and Manrope.

**Self-contained:** its base and brand swatch values are built into the preview, so it doesn't depend on the system's final tokens.

**Controls:** presets, six bases, eighteen brand swatches plus a custom colour, sidebar style, colour intensity and font.

**Derived values:** button fill, brand text and sidebar colours are adjusted automatically to reach 4.5:1. The soft tint is the brand colour mixed into the surface at 11%.

**Output:** a one-line summary to paste into chat.
